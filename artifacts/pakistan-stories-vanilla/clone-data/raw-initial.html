Yemberzal · یَمبرزل · Rawalakot

A living archive of the Poonch valley, told in the voice of its stones.

Yemberzal documents the forgotten Sikh history of Kashmir through field research, photographs, maps and oral testimony. Ten monuments, ten recorded memories — in Pahari, Urdu and English.

One of Rawalakot’s most important surviving Sikh-period buildings.

One of two Sikh-era buildings forming the core of Nimra Tariq’s 2024 study. Its masonry combines construction typical of Sikh-period hill shrines with features read as closer to a samadhi. Local oral tradition still attaches legends to the building.
